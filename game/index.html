How to play

Marco Reyes is new in Sunshine City. Lena Cruz has three jobs for him:
1. First Wheels — steal a parked car (E) and bring it to the garage. $500.
2. Package Run — grab the package at the pier, deliver north in 90s. $800.
3. Lose the Heat — drop your wanted level to zero. $1,200.
Stealing and shooting raise your wanted level; police chase harder as it grows. If they hold you on foot, you're Busted and pay a $200 fine. Progress saves automatically.
